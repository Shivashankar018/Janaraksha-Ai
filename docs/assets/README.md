## Note on the login page photo

The photo of uniformed police officers on the login page was supplied by the team for the buildathon demo. Before any public deployment or reuse:

- confirm that you have permission to use the photo and to show the people in it;
- if the photo shows the State Emblem of India or official insignia, check that the use is allowed under the State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005 and department rules.

If you are not sure, replace the photo: change `GW_PHOTO` in `src/assets/gateway-photo.js` to another image (a JPEG data URL), or remove the `<figure class="gw-photo">` block in `src/js/features/portal.js`.
